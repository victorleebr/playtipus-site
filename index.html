Hybrid breeding

Every pet is built from six body parts, and each one carries hidden genes. Pair two pets and see what hatches: maybe a fox tail on a striped snout, maybe something nobody has seen yet.

Family trees

Every pet remembers where it came from. Open its family tree to see its parents, and which one gave it each part, from a rare fennec fox head to a legendary saiga tail.

A farm on your taskbar

Playtipus docks along the bottom of your screen while you work. Pets wander, sleep and get up to things. Peek in whenever you like, or just let them be.

Expeditions

Pick a party and send them off along the taskbar, right past your open apps. Keep working; they'll keep walking.

Coats

Patterns, colours and the occasional ridiculous pair of stripes. Breed for the look you want, then show them off.
