Gene 01 · Breeding

Five Parts, Two Parents

Head, body, legs, tail and a Special. A baby takes each part from one parent, a coin flip every time, then rolls that part’s rarity.

Now and then a hidden gene from a grandparent wakes up instead: here, a Fennec Fox or a Saiga. That’s not a mutation, it’s heredity: every part also carries the genes of the parents, grandparents and great-grandparents behind it. Click a side of a rung to choose yourself, or let the egg decide.

    Gene 02 · Hybrids

    The Best Pets Are Hybrids. So Are the Worst.

    A pure-bred is good, but every species has a weak part. Breed round it and you get a champion. Stack the weak parts on one unlucky baby and you get a pet only its owner could love.

    Gene 03 · Card Fights

    Your Pet Is Your Deck

    Swap a tail and you swap a card. The pet you breed is the deck you fight with, so the real skill is in the egg.

    • Head1 card
    • Legs2 cards: a front-leg card and a hind-leg card
    • Tail1 card
    • BodyThe species, its HP, and the Basic attack card
    • SpecialThe Ultimate, when its meter fills

    Two copies of the head, leg and tail cards make an 8-card deck. The Basic attack is always there.

    Gene 04 · Taskbar

    It Lives on Your Taskbar

    Playtipus is not another window to mind. It sits on top of the real Windows taskbar, a thin strip of farm and road, while you get on with your day.

    Search for something, answer a mail, write your code. Your pets keep walking underneath, past your open apps and your clock.
