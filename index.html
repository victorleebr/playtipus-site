Hybrid breeding

Every pet is built from six body parts, and each one carries hidden genes. Pair two pets and see what hatches: maybe a fox tail on a striped snout, maybe something nobody has seen yet.

Family trees

Every pet remembers where it came from. Open its family tree to see its parents, and which one gave it each part, from a rare fennec fox head to a legendary saiga tail.

A farm on your taskbar

Playtipus docks along the bottom of your screen while you work. Pets wander, sleep and get up to things. Peek in whenever you like, tuck it behind your other windows, or just let them be.

Expeditions

Pick a party of three and send them along your taskbar to the Meadow, the Old Quarry or the Riverbank. They hop logs, wade through puddles, cross streams, dig up finds and camp by the fire on long trips. Keep working; they'll keep walking.

Bosses and loot

Some trips end with a local creature that won't move out of the way: a Wild, Angry or even Mighty one. Beat it and your party heads home with a chest. The rarest chests are very rare.

Rare looks

Every pet carries a hidden shade gene. Breed toward the palest or deepest end of a line and you reach Pure and Perfect shades, which shine. Getting there takes patience, and that's the point.

Music while you work

A built-in lofi player with its own playlist, shuffle and volume, and a folder where you can drop your own music. Leave the farm running and let it keep you company.

In the works

Card battles

Every body part becomes a card. Breed a better head, get a better move. Your support pets step in at the right moment with a heal, a shield or a block.

Arenas

Run, Swim and Fight ladders with rivals, bosses every ten stages and a podium at the end.

Questions

What do I need to play?

A Windows PC. Playtipus runs in a slim window docked along your taskbar, and you can make it taller when you want a closer look.

When does it come out?

There's no date yet. First a small playtest with friends, then the Steam page and wishlists, then early access. The newsletter is the first place we'll announce each step.

How much will it cost?

The price isn't set yet. We'll share it with the Steam page.

Will it slow my computer down?

It shouldn't. It's built to run all day in the background, and every update is checked against a CPU and memory budget before it ships.

Do my pets keep going when I close it?

Yes. Your farm keeps ticking while you're away, and your pets will have been busy when you come back.
